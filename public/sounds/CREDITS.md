# Chime credits

All five chimes (`running`, `needs-you`, `review`, `done`, `error`, as `.m4a`) are built from vibraphone recordings, hard mallets, in the **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Sam Gossner.

- Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/master/Idiophones/Struck%20Idiophones/Vibraphone/Hard%20Mallets
- Files used: `Vibes_hard_D4_v2_rr1_Main.wav`, `Vibes_hard_F4_v2_rr1_Main.wav`, `Vibes_hard_A4_v2_rr1_Main.wav`, `Vibes_hard_C5_v2_rr1_Main.wav`, `Vibes_hard_E5_v2_rr1_Main.wav`
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The samples were pitched, strummed into short shapes, trimmed, faded and levelled by `scripts/samples/build-chimes.mjs` in the groovenfill repo. Attribution isn't required, but it's given here anyway.
